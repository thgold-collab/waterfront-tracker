Two Sundays

Rebuilt from scratch after every listing on the board was re-verified against a property page or a county record. Four houses survived that check — two on each route. Everything here was confirmed still for sale on 6 August.

What the re-check changed

Of 38 houses, 23 are still active. Two had already sold, two went under contract, and eleven could not be confirmed against a real listing page at all.

Both houses on the previous version of this card are gone: one had sold in May, one is a tear-down. Two more that looked good last week — Holly Trail in Gloucester and Pattersons View in Hayes — turned out to be off-market and a shared community pier respectively.

Not one house on the board has a confirmed water depth. That is the single thing worth resolving, and it is a phone call.

  5. 2:15

    New Point Comfort

    Nature Conservancy preserve · dawn to dusk, free

    An accessible boardwalk out over the salt marsh to an observation deck, looking across at the New Point Comfort lighthouse of 1805 — now stranded offshore on its own scrap of island because the point eroded away around it. 105 acres on the Atlantic Flyway, with nesting least terns in season.

    The most striking thing on either route.

  6. 3:15

    Bethel Beach

    Natural Area Preserve · Mathews County

    A wild Chesapeake beach where sand lies over peat, so the shore is studded with the stumps of a drowned loblolly pine forest. Piping plover and the threatened beach tiger beetle nest here. Nothing manicured about it, which is the appeal.

  7. 4:30

    Yorktown — optional

    On the way home over the Coleman Bridge

    You cross the York anyway, so the riverwalk, the beach and the battlefield are right there. Waterfront tables if anyone wants a drink before the last hour. Cut it if the day has run long.

  3. 11:15

    Irvington

    Lancaster County · Carter's Creek · ten minutes from Kilmarnock

    The prettiest village on the Neck. Walk the lanes and the Tides Inn grounds down to Carter's Creek. Lunch here — Trick Dog Cafe, The Office Bistro and Golden Eagle Grill are the names to try, and all of them want a phone call on a Sunday.

  4. 1:15

    Windmill Point

    Westland Beach & public fishing pier · end of Windmill Point Rd

    The mouth of the river, where the Rappahannock gives out into the Chesapeake. A recently rebuilt county park with restored beach and a long accessible fishing pier — public, free, and about as much open water as Virginia offers in one view. You can see the Norris Bridge from here, which saves driving it.

  6. 3:45

    Reedville Main Street

    Northumberland County · the historic district

    Walk Main Street for the Victorian houses built on menhaden money — at the turn of the century this was said to be one of the wealthiest towns per head in the country, and the street still shows it. The Fishermen's Museum at 504 Main is worth a return trip but is closed Sundays, so this is a streetscape stop rather than a museum one.

Before you go

  • All four houses are drive‑bys unless you call. Sunday showings out here need an appointment, and the call is also how you get the depth figures.
  • Phone the lunch stop. Mathews Grill & Deli lists Sunday hours; Irvington's places do not reliably. Two Urbanna restaurants have closed since their listings were written.
  • Drive times are estimates from the tracker, not independently measured.
  • Option A has the better houses; Option B has the better towns and is half an hour shorter. If the point of the day is deciding whether you want to live out there at all, B answers that better.

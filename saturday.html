Why the packed lunch changes everything

The version of this day with a restaurant in it was pinned to an eleven o'clock opening, which pushed the start late and cost an hour in the middle. Carrying lunch removes the only fixed time in the day. Nothing here opens or closes; you can run twenty minutes over anywhere and take it back later.

So the day got denser and shorter at once: 4h35 of driving instead of 5h23, and it now takes in seven towns and four houses instead of five and one. Out on US‑33 through Middlesex, over the Norris Bridge, down the Neck and home on US‑360 across the river again at Tappahannock — where the last two houses happen to be, an hour and five minutes from your own front door.

  1. 7:00

    Depart Glen Allen — I‑64 East, then US‑33 East through West Point

  2. 8:15 drive-by

    Saluda

    Middlesex County seat · ten minutes

    The road goes through it. Courthouse green, a couple of blocks, and you have seen what the administrative side of county life looks like out here. Coffee is eight minutes further on, which is the only reason this is brief.

  3. 8:35 walk it

    Urbanna — and coffee

    Middlesex County · a colonial tobacco port since the 1680s · an hour and five

    The longest walk of the day, and the right one to give the time to. One of Virginia's original official ports, still small enough to cover end to end on foot. Coffee first, then Cross Street down to the harbour on Urbanna Creek, past the 1766 tobacco warehouse.

    The Wooden Pickle — coffee, baked goods, sandwiches
    Saturday 6:00 am – 8:00 pm
    Open from six, so it does not matter how early you leave. Note it is closed Wednesdays and Thursdays — Saturday is one of its good days, which is luck rather than planning. It appears to have taken over 51 Cross Street, the premises of the Cross Street Coffee & Cafe that closed, though one listing still gives its address as 280 Virginia St. Both are the same two blocks; the pin searches by name so it will find it either way.
    Hours from a Yelp listing updated May 2026 and the cafe's own Facebook page, not from a call.
    Backups in town: Big Oak Cafe (Sat 7:30 am – 1:30 pm) and Virginia Street Cafe (Sat 8 am – 8 pm).
    The Wooden Pickle →

    This is the town on your list that most rewards being judged on foot rather than on a map. Whether you would happily spend a Saturday here is a question only the sidewalk answers — and it is the useful contrast with Irvington, ninety minutes from now.

  4. 10:05 walk it

    White Stone — over the bridge

    Lancaster County · twenty-five minutes, village and house together

    The crossing comes first and the crossing is the event: up over the Norris Bridge, nearly two miles, and the Rappahannock opens out underneath you close to its widest. White Stone at the far end is a crossroads village and twenty minutes is honestly its right measure — then the cheapest house of the day is three minutes away.

  5. 10:35 walk it

    Irvington

    Lancaster County · Carter's Creek · thirty-five minutes

    Four minutes from White Stone and the prettiest village on the Neck. Walk the lanes and the Tides Inn grounds down to Carter's Creek. If Urbanna is the working port, this is the postcard, and holding the two of them in your head an hour apart is the most useful thing the day gives you.

  7. 12:00 walk it

    Kilmarnock — and lunch out of the boot

    Main Street · thirty minutes

    The practical town of the group and the answer to "but where would we actually buy anything". Main Street on foot for twenty minutes settles it. This is also where lunch lands on the clock — though if you would rather eat somewhere prettier, the town dock at Tappahannock is on the river at about half past one and worth holding out for. I have not verified a picnic table anywhere on this route, so plan on a bench or the car either way.

  8. 1:15 drive-by

    Warsaw

    Richmond County seat · US‑360 runs through it · ten minutes

    Forty-five minutes up from Kilmarnock and the road goes straight through. A third small county seat, and the useful thing about it is that nobody out here is selling it to you.

  9. 1:35 walk it

    Tappahannock — two houses and the river

    Essex County · where US‑360 crosses the Rappahannock · fifty minutes

    The surprise of the exercise. Tappahannock is the closest town on your whole list — an hour and five minutes from home — and it holds two waterfront houses in the low four hundreds. It is also your second crossing of the Rappahannock, forty miles upstream from the first, which is the geography of the day in two views.

What to settle from the kerb

None of this needs an appointment, which is the point. Tick them off as you go.

Before you go

  • Leaving earlier just lengthens the front of the day. Nothing on this card opens or closes except coffee, and that is open from six. Leave at 6:30 and you get an extra half hour in Urbanna, or Deltaville, or both Kilmarnock houses at a walk instead of a glance.
  • Every house here is a drive-by, and that is deliberate. Saturday showings need an appointment; the questions on this card do not. If one of them survives the kerb, that is when you ring.
  • What you are giving up: the Reedville end. Two houses up there (878 Forest Green, 65, new-build 2023 on the Little Wicomico; 2082 Fairport Rd, 61, boat lift and everything replaced) plus the Fishermen's Museum, open Saturdays 11–4. It is a 2h04 run each way and adding it costs the four o'clock. It makes an easy Saturday on its own.
  • Drive times are routed door to door, not estimated — 4h35 over 181 miles. No allowance for traffic or a slow left onto US‑360, which is where the half hour of slack goes.
  • Two pins are street-level, not house-level. Peach Tree Ln and Pine Reach Dr did not resolve to a house number in the geocoder I checked against; everything else did. Glance at those two when Maps opens them.
